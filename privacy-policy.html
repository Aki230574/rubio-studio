Privacy Policy — Rubio Studio

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Rubio Studio ("the app", "I", "me") is a personal publishing and analytics tool built and operated by Jose A. Rubio-Matias, an independent content creator based in Austin, Texas, USA. It connects to one TikTok account only: my own (@josea.rubio). It has no other users, no public sign-up, and no advertising.

This policy explains, plainly, what the app accesses through the TikTok API, why, where it is kept, and how to remove it.

1. Who this applies to

The app is used only by me, for my own TikTok account. No other person's TikTok account is connected. If anyone else ever authorized the app by mistake, their data would be deleted as described in section 7.

2. Data the app accesses

When I sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approve the requested permissions, the app can access:

Permission (scope) Data Why
user.info.basic Open ID, display name, avatar To confirm which account is connected and show it on the posting screen.
user.info.stats Follower count, following count, total likes, video count To build my own daily growth reports.
video.list My public videos and their metadata (ID, caption, cover image, duration, creation time, view, like, comment and share counts) To track how my own videos perform.
video.upload Ability to send a video to my TikTok inbox as a draft So I can finish and publish it myself in the TikTok app.
video.publish Ability to publish a video to my profile, with caption and settings I choose To post my own original videos after I approve each one.

The app also stores the OAuth access token and refresh token that TikTok issues, so it can keep working without asking me to sign in every day.

The app does not access other users' data, direct messages, contacts, location, payment information, or any data beyond the scopes above.

3. How the data is used

  • To show me statistics about my own account and videos (reports and charts).
  • To upload and publish my own original videos, and only after I have reviewed and approved the specific video, caption and settings.
  • To check the status of an upload so I know when a post is live.

The data is not used for advertising, profiling, selling, training AI models, or any purpose other than the ones listed here.

4. AI assistant

I use an AI assistant to help me prepare drafts (captions, posting times) and summarize my statistics. The assistant runs in a private, access-controlled workspace that only I use. It never publishes anything without my explicit approval for that specific post.

5. Storage and security

  • Tokens and statistics are stored on a private server that only I control, protected by access controls and encryption in transit (HTTPS).
  • The TikTok client secret and tokens are never published, embedded in public code, or shared with anyone.
  • Video files are kept only as long as needed to complete an upload.

6. Sharing and selling

I do not sell, rent, or share TikTok data with third parties. The only exceptions are the infrastructure providers that host the private server (which process data only to provide that service), or if disclosure is required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Statistics are kept for up to 24 months so I can compare performance over time, then deleted.
  • Tokens are kept only while the connection is active. Access tokens expire after 24 hours and refresh tokens after 365 days (TikTok's limits).
  • When access is revoked, the app deletes the stored tokens within 7 days and stops collecting data.
  • Any data from an account other than mine is deleted immediately when discovered.

8. Revoking access

You can disconnect the app at any time:

  • In the TikTok app: Profile → Menu (☰) → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions, then remove Rubio Studio.
  • Or by emailing aki@agentmail.to; I will revoke the token through TikTok's revoke endpoint and delete the stored data.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by its adult owner.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
