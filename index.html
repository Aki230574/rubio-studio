Rubio Studio

Rubio Studio is the publishing and analytics workspace of Jose A. Rubio-Matias, a Spanish-language creator who makes short videos about artificial intelligence and AI agents (YouTube @JoseARubio, TikTok @josea.rubio).

What it does

  • Performance reports: reads follower and video statistics for my own TikTok account and turns them into daily reports.
  • Approved publishing: uploads my own original videos to TikTok. I review the video, caption, privacy level and interaction settings, and nothing is posted until I approve that specific post.

Who can use it

Rubio Studio is a single-user tool. It is connected only to my own TikTok account. There is no public sign-up.
